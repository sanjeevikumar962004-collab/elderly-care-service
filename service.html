Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up verified nursing care for your loved ones in Salem.

Are your Salem nurses background checked and medically licensed?

keyboard_arrow_down
Yes, 100%. Every nurse deployed by Stackly Salem holds valid credentials from the Tamil Nadu Nurses and Midwives Council (TNC) with verified B.Sc Nursing or GNM certifications. Furthermore, we conduct strict local police verification and background checks across Salem before any home assignment.

Is in-home nursing care covered by medical health insurance?

keyboard_arrow_down
Many leading private health insurance providers and corporate TPA policies in India now reimburse domiciliary hospitalization and post-operative home nursing under specific conditions. Our billing desk provides comprehensive clinical notes, stamped doctor prescriptions, and GST-compliant invoices to streamline your reimbursement claim.

How does the nurse coordinate with our family doctor or hospital?

keyboard_arrow_down
Our nurses strictly follow the treatment regimen established by your treating physician. At each shift transition, vitals and medical charts are logged digitally. If any parameter departs from normal thresholds, our duty clinical lead immediately communicates with your family physician for dose titrations or urgent adjustments.

What is your response time for emergency care deployment in Salem?

keyboard_arrow_down
For planned post-discharge care, we deploy nursing staff within 4 to 6 hours across Salem city. For urgent and critical stabilization requests in locations such as Fairlands, Hasthampatti, Junction, or Ammapet, our rapid response clinical mobile team typically arrives within 18 to 30 minutes with oxygen and emergency diagnostic gear.

Can we change our assigned nurse if the patient is uncomfortable?

keyboard_arrow_down
Patient dignity and comfort are paramount. If for any reason your loved one feels uncomfortable with the assigned nurse or caregiver, our patient care coordinator will arrange a replacement within 12 hours with zero penalty or administrative disruption.
