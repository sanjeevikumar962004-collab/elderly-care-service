Salem Center Access & Care Protocols

Frequently asked questions regarding patient admissions, home nursing start times, and center visits.

How fast can a nurse arrive at our Salem residence?

For critical vitals and urgent medication needs within Salem city limits (Fairlands, Hasthampatti, Alagapuram, Shevapet), our certified staff responds within 45 to 90 minutes. Planned 12/24-hour shifts deploy within 6 hours of initial assessment.

check_circle Fastest turn-around in Salem
receipt_long

What documents should we bring for a clinic consultation?

Please bring recent hospital discharge summaries, current prescription lists, diagnostic reports (blood tests, ECG, CT/MRI), and patient identification. This allows our Medical Director to design a flawless nursing protocol.

fact_check Digital uploads also accepted
accessible_forward

Is the Salem Center wheelchair-accessible with parking?

Yes. Our Fairlands/Junction center features step-free ramp access, dedicated patient transit bays, stretcher lift access, and complimentary reserved patient parking directly in front of the clinical wing.
